# M7 manual checks

Automated: recorder sync with a sound card running 2 % fast stays within
2 frames, pause, sound-only and retake (`cargo test -p rw-capture`); the
Record tab, countdown, recording window and timeline placement
(`cargo test -p rw-ui --test record`).

The acceptance run needs real hardware, so it is done by hand on each OS.

## Acceptance: 30-minute screen + webcam + microphone

1. Open a project. Library → Record. Screen: the screen; Camera: the
   webcam; Microphone: a real microphone. The meter moves when you speak.
2. Start recording. After the 3-2-1 countdown, clap once in view of the
   camera, close to the microphone.
3. Leave it running for 30 minutes with a clock or a video playing on the
   screen. Pause for about 10 s once, then resume.
4. At about 30 minutes, clap again in view of the camera. Stop.
5. Check:
   - The screen clip is on the main track and the camera clip is above it
     in the bottom-right corner, both starting at the same point and the
     same length to within a frame or two.
   - Step frame by frame to each clap: the clap's sound (waveform spike)
     and the camera frame where the hands meet are within 2 frames, both
     at the start and at the end.
   - `Recordings/` in the project folder holds "Recording N - Screen.mp4"
     and "Recording N - Camera.mp4".
   - The recorded time shown matched the clip length (the pause isn't in
     the clip).

## Permissions and failures

- **Windows:** Settings → Privacy & security → Camera (then Microphone)
  → turn off for desktop apps. Choosing the microphone in the Record tab
  shows a message saying what to change; starting with a blocked camera
  shows one too, and the app keeps working. Turn access back on, press
  Refresh devices, and recording works.
- **Windows:** start a recording with another app holding the camera: a
  message says it's in use.
- **macOS:** the first recording asks for Screen Recording, Camera and
  Microphone access. Deny one: a message points to System Settings →
  Privacy & Security. Allow it, restart the app if macOS asks, and record.
- **Linux:** screen capture works on X11 (and XWayland); under a pure
  Wayland session x11grab shows a black or failed screen.
- Unplug the camera while recording: recording stops by itself, keeps
  what was captured and says why.
- Retake throws the take away (no files left in `Recordings/`) and counts
  down again.
- Kill the app mid-recording (Task Manager / `kill -9`): the files in
  `Recordings/` still play up to the last second or so.
