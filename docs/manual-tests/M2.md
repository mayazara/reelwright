# M2 manual checks

Automated tests cover the edit commands, decoders, compositor, export and
playback clock. These steps check what needs eyes and ears.

## Performance soak (acceptance: 3 video + 2 audio at 1080p30, drift < 1 frame over 10 min)

```sh
RW_PERF=1 RW_SOAK_SECS=600 cargo test --release -p rw-engine --test playback soak -- --nocapture
```

Expect `dropped` under 1 % and `max lag` under 33 ms. Record the result and
machine in `PROGRESS.md`.

## Editing pass

1. Create a 16:9 project, import a video with sound, an image and a song.
2. Drag the video to the timeline, then the image before it: the image lands
   first and the video moves right (magnetic main track).
3. Drag the song below the tracks: a new audio track appears with a waveform.
4. Press Space: picture and sound play together; the meter moves.
5. Put the playhead in the video, press S, select the right half, press Del:
   the gap closes. Ctrl+Z restores it.
6. Drag a clip's right edge: it snaps to the playhead and other edges; hold
   Ctrl to disable snapping.
7. Right-click the video → Detach audio: a linked audio clip appears; moving
   one moves both.
8. Press M to add a marker; drag it on the ruler; ↑/↓ jump between edits.
9. Click Export, pick a file, wait for "Exported …", play the file in any
   player and compare with the preview.
