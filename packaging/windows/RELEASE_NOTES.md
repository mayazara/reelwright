## What's in this version

- **Editing:** multi-track timeline, trimming, splitting, speed (0.1–16×, sound keeps its pitch), reverse and freeze frames.
- **Look:** transform and crop, colour adjustments, 24 filters, blend modes, 16 effects including green screen, and 20 transitions.
- **Text:** titles, lower thirds, quote cards, countdowns and rolling credits, with 33 bundled fonts and animated entrances (Arabic, Hindi, Chinese/Japanese/Korean and emoji supported).
- **Graphics:** 450+ emoji and stickers (OpenMoji), shapes and arrows, audio visualisers, sound effects, and a brand kit for your colours, fonts and logos.
- **Export:** MP4 (H.264, HEVC, AV1 where your graphics card supports them), WebM, GIF and MP3, from 480p to 4K, using your computer's hardware encoder when it has one.

## Download for Windows

- **Reelwright-…-setup.exe** — double-click to install. No administrator rights needed; adds Start-menu and desktop shortcuts and can be removed from *Settings → Apps*.
- **Reelwright-…-portable.zip** — unzip anywhere and run `Reelwright.exe`. Nothing is installed.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. No other downloads: video codecs (FFmpeg), fonts and filters are included, and it works without a dedicated graphics card.

**First run:** this build isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

Everything runs offline; the app never connects to the internet.
