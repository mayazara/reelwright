## What's in this version

- **Editing:** multi-track timeline, trimming, splitting, speed (0.1–16×, sound keeps its pitch), reverse and freeze frames.
- **Look:** transform and crop, colour adjustments, 24 filters, blend modes, 16 effects including green screen, and 20 transitions.
- **Text:** titles, lower thirds, quote cards, countdowns and rolling credits, with 33 bundled fonts and animated entrances (Arabic, Hindi, Chinese/Japanese/Korean and emoji supported).
- **Graphics:** 450+ emoji and stickers (OpenMoji), shapes and arrows, audio visualisers, sound effects, and a brand kit for your colours, fonts and logos.
- **Recording:** record your screen, webcam, microphone and computer sound, with a 3-2-1 countdown, pause and retake. Recordings are saved with the project and land on the timeline, with the webcam as a corner overlay.
- **Export:** MP4 (H.264, HEVC, AV1 where your graphics card supports them), WebM, GIF and MP3, from 480p to 4K, using your computer's hardware encoder when it has one.

## Downloads

**Windows** (64-bit Windows 10 or 11)
- **Reelwright-…-windows-x64-setup.exe** — double-click to install. No administrator rights needed; adds Start-menu and desktop shortcuts and can be removed from *Settings → Apps*.
- **Reelwright-…-windows-x64-portable.zip** — unzip anywhere and run `Reelwright.exe`. Nothing is installed.

**macOS**
- **Reelwright-…-macos-arm64.dmg** for Apple Silicon (M1 and later), **…-macos-x86_64.dmg** for Intel Macs. Open it and drag Reelwright to Applications.

**Linux** (x86-64, 2022 distributions or newer)
- **Reelwright-…-linux-x86_64.AppImage** — make it executable and run it; nothing is installed.
- **reelwright_…_amd64.deb** — for Ubuntu, Debian and similar: `sudo apt install ./reelwright_…_amd64.deb`.

No other downloads are needed: video codecs (FFmpeg), fonts and filters are included, and it works without a dedicated graphics card.

**First run:** builds that aren't code-signed yet trigger a warning. On Windows, SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, right-click Reelwright in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again. Recording asks for camera, microphone and (on macOS) screen-recording permission the first time.

Everything runs offline; the app never connects to the internet.
